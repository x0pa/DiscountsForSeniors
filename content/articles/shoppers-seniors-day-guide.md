If Thursday is not already marked on your calendar, this guide may change that. Every Thursday, Shoppers Drug Mart runs a seniors day that gives shoppers aged 65 and older 20% off regular priced merchandise in stores across Canada. In Quebec, the same chain operates as Pharmaprix, and the same Thursday habit applies there. It is one of the largest and most reliable discounts in our [directory](/directory/), and it rewards a little planning. A purchase held for two days can cost a fifth less, and a cart planned around the rules can save far more than a cart filled at random.

## How does seniors day work?

The rule is simple. You shop on a Thursday, you show your PC Optimum card, and the discount comes off eligible items at the till. The card matters. The discount is tied to PC Optimum, so the card or the app on your phone needs to be yours, and it needs to be scanned during the transaction. Borrowing the card of a family member will not work for this offer, even if that person is with you in the store. If you do not yet have a card, you can join PC Optimum at the store or in the app before you shop, and it costs nothing to join. Once your card is scanned, the saving is automatic. There is no coupon to clip and no code to remember.

## What does the discount cover?

The 20% applies to regular priced items only. That single rule shapes the whole trip. An item at its usual price qualifies, and an item already marked down in the flyer or on a shelf tag does not get a second reduction. That does not make sale items a poor choice. It simply means they follow a separate logic. Buy the regular priced goods on Thursday and let the flyer handle the rest of your list on any day that suits you. The discount reaches a broad stretch of the store, from vitamins and toiletries to household goods, snacks, cosmetics and small electronics, as long as each item sits at its regular price when you reach the till.

## What does the discount never cover?

Some categories never receive the discount, on any Thursday, in any store. Prescriptions are excluded. Tobacco is excluded. Lottery tickets, gift cards and prepaid cards are excluded, and so are transit tickets and transit passes. This list is part of the published terms of the offer, and staff at the till cannot override it for a single purchase. Knowing the list in advance saves disappointment and helps you plan. Save your Thursday effort for the parts of the store where the 20% truly applies, because those savings are real and they add up month after month.

## A note on age

The headline age for the day is 65, but it is not the whole story in every location. Some stores set a lower age for seniors day, which means shoppers in their late fifties or early sixties may qualify at a particular store even though the national advertising says 65 and older. Policies can differ from store to store, so if you are between 55 and 64, it is worth asking at your own store before Thursday arrives. Ask once, note the answer, and shop with confidence from then on. Staff at the cosmetics counter or at customer service can usually confirm the local rule in a moment.

## When Thursday gets bigger

On certain Thursdays, the chain runs Super Seniors Days. These are the ordinary seniors day plus a bonus. The bonus changes from one event to the next, and forms have included bonus PC Optimum points or a gift card given with a minimum spend. The 20% discount still applies on those days, so a Super Seniors Day is the best Thursday of all to bring a longer list. The flyer and the app announce these dates in advance. If you see one coming, it can be worth holding larger purchases of regular priced goods for that week rather than buying them on a plain Thursday.

## How the savings stack

The discount and the points work together, and that is where careful shoppers do especially well. You present your card, the 20% comes off each eligible item, and you still earn PC Optimum points on the amount you actually pay. Points earned on a Thursday shop can grow when the card also carries personal offers from the app. Those personal offers apply to specific products, and when one of them matches an item on your Thursday list, the same purchase can earn points while its price falls by a fifth. Open the app before you leave home, load any offers that match your list, and let the two savings meet in the same cart. Our [Shoppers Drug Mart directory entry](/discounts/shoppers-seniors-day/) keeps the core terms in one place for a quick check before you go.

## How to plan the trip

The shoppers who save the most treat Thursday as the end of a small weekly routine. Keep a running list during the week, on paper or on your phone, and add each front store item as you notice it running low at home. Toothpaste, shampoo, vitamins, cleaning supplies and batteries all wait patiently for Thursday. Hold those purchases rather than buying them on the day you notice them. Use flyer sales through the week for fresh food, sale priced goods and the categories the discount skips. Then, on Thursday, walk in with a list that was built for the offer, buy what is on it, and leave before the cart fills with extras the plan never called for.
