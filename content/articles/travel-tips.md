Travel is one of the best ways to stay active, meet new people and create lasting memories. With a little planning, Canadian seniors can enjoy amazing experiences without overspending. Here are ten tips to help you travel smarter and safer.

1. **Look for senior discounts.** Many airlines, hotels and attractions offer special rates for seniors 55+. Always ask and don't be shy about showing your age.
2. **Travel during the off season.** You'll find lower prices, fewer crowds and a more relaxed experience. Spring and fall are great times to explore popular destinations.
3. **Consider travel insurance.** It gives you peace of mind if plans change or unexpected things happen. Choose a plan that covers health, trip cancellations and emergency care.

> The best travel plans aren't about where you go, but how good you feel along the way.

4. **Pack light and smart.** Bring versatile clothing, comfortable shoes and only the essentials. A lighter bag means less stress and more freedom to explore.
5. **Stay connected.** Keep in touch with family and friends. A local SIM card or portable Wi-Fi can help you find your way and share your adventures.
6. **Be open to new experiences.** Try local food, meet new people and say yes to new activities. Some of the best memories come from the unexpected.
7. **Keep your health in mind.** Stay hydrated, get enough rest and take your medications. A little preparation goes a long way in keeping you feeling your best.
8. **Take advantage of senior programs.** Many destinations offer senior friendly tours, special events and group travel options. It's a great way to save money and make new friends.
9. **Use technology to your advantage.** Download useful travel apps, check the weather and keep digital copies of important documents. It makes travel easier and safer.
10. **Enjoy the journey.** It's not just about the destination. Take time to enjoy the views, the people and the little moments along the way.
