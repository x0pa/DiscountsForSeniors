Yes, RONA does offer a senior discount, and it is one of the more generous age offers in Canadian home improvement retail. Shoppers aged 55 and older save 10% on regular priced products at RONA and RONA+ stores, but only on one day each month, and only at stores that take part. That timing is the catch and the opportunity at once. Miss the day and you wait a month. Plan for the day and a large project shop costs noticeably less.

## When does the discount run?

The discount runs on the first Tuesday of every month. That is the only day it runs. There is no seniors day in other weeks and no standing discount on other days of the month for shoppers who miss it. The qualifying age is 55, which is younger than the age used by many other chains in our directory. If you have a project coming up, the calendar becomes part of the budget. A purchase list finished in the final week of one month can wait a few days for the Tuesday that follows, and the saving on a cart of regular priced products can be well worth the short wait.

## What do you need at the till?

Bring photo ID that shows your date of birth. Staff will ask for it at the till, because the discount depends on age and the store needs to confirm that you meet the threshold of 55. A licence or another official card with your date of birth printed on it will do the job. Keep it reachable rather than buried at the bottom of a bag, especially on a busy Tuesday morning when the line behind you is long. The check is quick, it happens once per transaction, and it soon becomes as routine as presenting a loyalty card at any other shop.

## Where the offer applies

Not every RONA or RONA+ location takes part. The offer runs at participating stores, and participation can vary, so the store near you may or may not be included this month. Before a large shop, confirm that your store participates. A short phone call, a question at customer service on an earlier visit, or a check of the store page online will settle it. Confirming in advance matters more here than at chains where the offer is universal, because driving across town with a project list on the wrong Tuesday is a frustration you only need to experience once.

## The long list of exclusions

This is the part of the offer to read twice. The exclusion list is long, and it covers many of the largest purchases in the store. Major appliances do not receive the discount. Construction lumber is excluded, and so are asphalt shingles and gypsum panels, three staples of renovation work. Gift cards are excluded, as they are in nearly every age offer in Canada. Installation services and delivery services are also excluded, which matters when a project quote mixes products with labour. On top of those categories, many named brands are excluded from the offer, and the brands can cover specific products that a shopper would reasonably expect to include. Ask about a costly brand name item before Tuesday, not while the line waits behind you at the till, so there are no surprises in the total.

## Can the discount be combined with other offers?

The RONA discount cannot be combined with other offers. If an item is part of a separate promotion, you will generally need to choose which saving applies rather than apply both to the same purchase. Sometimes the other promotion will be the better deal, especially on a single large item, and sometimes the 10% on a full cart of regular priced products will win by a wide margin. Do the simple math before Tuesday arrives. Add up the regular priced goods the discount can touch, take a tenth off that total in your head, and compare it with what the other offer would save you on the same list.

## The rival that says no

It helps to know how unusual an offer like this is among the large home improvement chains. Home Depot Canada, the biggest rival in this category, has no senior discount at all, on any day, at any age. We set out the details in our guide to [stores with no senior discount](/articles/no-senior-discount/), where Home Depot sits alongside Costco and Walmart. That contrast makes the RONA Tuesday worth protecting in your routine. A modest percentage, offered reliably each month by a national chain, is a genuine saving in a category where many shoppers receive no age based consideration whatsoever.

## How to plan a project around it

The best use of this offer is a planned project shop rather than a scattered one. Spend the month collecting a list for one project, a bathroom refresh, a garden build, a room repainted, and price the whole list before the Tuesday arrives. Save the purchases that qualify for that single trip, so the 10% lands on as much of the project as possible at once. Price the excluded categories elsewhere in the meantime, since lumber, appliances and panels will cost the same at RONA on Tuesday as on any other day, and a rival store may beat that price on its own promotion. Buy services such as delivery and installation on their own merits, separate from the discount math. Then arrive on the first Tuesday with your ID, your list and your measurements in hand, confirm the total item by item as it scans, and enjoy the rare feeling of a renovation that started under budget.
