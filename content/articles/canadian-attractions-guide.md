Some of the best days out in Canada already carry a lower price for seniors. You just have to know where that price lives and choose it when you buy. This guide brings together six places from our directory, from two Toronto landmarks to a national pass, a bridge park in British Columbia, a dinosaur museum in Alberta, and a cinema chain in four provinces. Every price below comes from the entry for that place, checked against the operator own published policy on October 18, 2026. All six start at age 65.

## What does a senior pay at the Toronto Zoo?

The Toronto Zoo sells a Senior ticket to every guest aged 65 and older, and the zoo publishes its full price list on its own site. A senior pays $38 in peak season against $45 for an adult, and $30.50 in the off season against $35, so the saving holds all year. Peak season runs from May 2 to October 31, and the off season runs from November 1 to May 1. All prices are plus tax. Child tickets cover ages 3 to 12 and cost $32 in peak season and $25 in the off season.

Buy the Senior ticket on the zoo website or at the gate, and bring photo ID in case staff ask at entry. Weekday mornings in the off season are the calmest time to visit, and your ticket costs the least then as well. If you visit often, the zoo also publishes Senior memberships beside its daily rates. See the full terms in our [Toronto Zoo entry](/discounts/toronto-zoo-senior-tickets/).

## What does a senior pay at the CN Tower?

The CN Tower publishes a Senior price on every ticket it sells, for guests aged 65 and older. The most common choice is Timed General Admission, at $47 for an adult and $34 for a senior. The Top plus Timed General Admission costs $59 and $46. A Flex ticket costs $62 and $49. Sea the Sky, which adds entry to Ripley's Aquarium and is sold online only, costs $79 and $54.25. Premier costs $99 and $76, and the VIP Tour costs $199 and $176. Children aged 2 and under enter free, and taxes are extra on all prices.

The saving grows with the ticket, from $13 on Timed General Admission to $24.75 on Sea the Sky. Book ahead if you can, since tickets can be booked up to 30 days in advance, and bring photo ID in case staff ask when you arrive. See the full list in our [CN Tower entry](/discounts/cn-tower-senior-tickets/).

## What does the Parks Canada Discovery Pass cost a senior?

The Discovery Pass is the annual pass from Parks Canada, and it carries a published senior price for anyone aged 65 and older. The pass costs $71.50 for a senior against $83.50 for an adult, and a Family or Group pass costs $167.50. The pass stays valid for 12 months and opens the gate at national parks, national historic sites, and national marine conservation areas across the country.

Daily admission carries a senior price too, at $10.75 against $12.25 for an adult, and youth aged 17 and under enter free. Add up your likely visits before you choose. Two or three visits to sites with daily fees can already cover the senior pass price. See the details in our [Parks Canada entry](/discounts/parks-canada-discovery-pass/).

## What does a senior pay at Capilano Suspension Bridge?

Capilano Suspension Bridge Park in North Vancouver includes a senior rate in its published ticket prices. The park counts an adult as 18 to 64 and a senior as 65 and up, and in each rate class the senior price sits about $5 under the adult price. The bridge itself, the cliffwalk, and the treetop walkways all sit inside one admission, so the saving applies to the full visit.

There is no single fixed daytime price at this park. Prices are set by date and vary by season and by rate type, so check the price calendar on the park own site for your day. The published example in our entry is the Summer Twilight rate, for entry after 5 p.m., where an adult pays $59.95 and a senior pays $55.45. Children aged 0 to 5 enter free. See the full notes in our [Capilano entry](/discounts/capilano-suspension-bridge-senior/).

## What does a senior pay at the Royal Tyrrell Museum?

The Royal Tyrrell Museum in Drumheller is where Canadians go to stand next to some of the finest dinosaur fossils found anywhere in the world, and its published prices give seniors a clear saving. The museum counts an adult as 18 to 64 and a senior as 65 and up, and a senior pays $16 where an adult pays $25, a saving of $9 on every visit.

The full published price list runs adult $25.00, senior $16.00, youth aged 7 to 17 at $12.00, and family admission at $55.00. Children aged 6 and under enter free, although a ticket is still required for them, and every price includes tax. From September 1 to May 14, the museum opens Tuesday to Sunday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and closes on Mondays, except for holiday Mondays. From May 15 to August 31, it opens daily from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. See the full terms in our [Royal Tyrrell Museum entry](/discounts/royal-tyrrell-museum-senior/).

## What do seniors save at Landmark Cinemas?

Landmark Cinemas sells a Senior ticket to guests aged 65 and older, and the category appears on the official page of every theatre checked, in Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, and Saskatchewan. Landmark sets the price at each theatre rather than for the chain, so there is no single national senior price to print. What we can print is the range the theatres themselves publish.

At the Waterloo, Ontario theatre, senior prices run from $9.75 to $11.25 with the online booking fee counted in, against regular prices of $13.00 to $14.50. Other theatres checked, among them Caledon, West Kelowna, Regina, Kingston, Calgary, Saskatoon, and London, fall in a similar range. On Mondays, every theatre page checked publishes 20% off admission and concessions for Senior's Day. There is no booking fee at the box office or the kiosk. See the full notes in our [Landmark Cinemas entry](/discounts/landmark-cinemas-senior-pricing/).

## How should seniors plan an attractions day?

Bring photo ID that shows your age on every visit, since staff can ask to see it at entry. Choose the Senior ticket or the Senior pass at the moment you buy, and check the attraction own ticket page before a special trip, since prices and hours can change. Note which prices add tax and which ones include it. Royal Tyrrell prices include tax, while Toronto Zoo and CN Tower prices are plus tax. When a programme changes, we check it again and update the listing. [Tell us](/contact/) if you spot a change first.
