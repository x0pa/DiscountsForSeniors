Some senior discounts are pleasant surprises. One of these two is better than a surprise: it is a date you can circle on the calendar and plan around. The other is a warning about how discount claims travel from site to site. M&M Food Market publishes the terms of its seniors day for the whole chain, so you can plan around them with confidence. Giant Tiger publishes no chain wide seniors day policy at all, so any answer that gives it a set age, a set percentage and a set day came from somewhere other than the chain itself. Both stores are worth your money. Only one of them makes a promise we can verify.

## Giant Tiger and the missing policy

Giant Tiger is a Canadian discount store chain with deep roots in smaller towns, the sort of store where a single trip can cover socks, soap, storage bins and a birthday card. Its stores run as franchises, and that structure explains everything that follows.

Search for a Giant Tiger seniors day and you will find the same settled answer repeated everywhere: a monthly day, a set age, a set percentage taken off select products. It sounds official. It appears on no Giant Tiger page. The chain publishes no seniors day policy: no age, no percentage and no day. The only trace on Giant Tiger's own help property is a customer describing two stores that ran a seniors day on different days, which is exactly what you would expect when each franchise store sets its own schedule.

So the honest answer is local. Some Giant Tiger stores run a seniors day of their own, and others may not run one at all. Where one runs, the store sets the day, the age and the saving, and the only way to learn them is to ask at the store where you shop. Four short questions cover it: does this store run a seniors day, on what day, at what age, and for what saving?

A list kept through the month still pays off at Giant Tiger. Note the towel that frays and the cleaning staple that runs low, and buy them when it suits you, or on the day your own store names if it runs one. Clothing and household goods rarely need to be bought the moment you notice them, and that patience serves you with or without a discount day attached.

## M&M Food Market and every Tuesday

M&M Food Market is the frozen food chain, built around meals and ingredients that wait in the freezer until the day you need them. Every Tuesday, shoppers aged 60 and older save 10% on regular priced items, in store and online. That weekly rhythm is easier to build into a routine than a monthly date, and it suits the way many households shop for frozen food, in smaller amounts more often.

For online orders, place the order on Tuesday so the saving applies to that order. M&M publishes no promo code for this offer, so treat any code you find copied on another site as a reason to double check rather than a fact. Proof of age is required, gift cards are excluded, and the offer does not apply at M&M Food Market Express counters inside convenience stores. That last point is easy to miss, because the name above the counter looks familiar even though the counter itself sits inside another store.

A freezer list works best when it stays short and honest. Look inside before you order, note the meals you will actually cook this week, and replace what you used last week. Frozen food keeps, but it still takes space, and a freezer packed without a plan has a way of hiding dinner rather than providing it. The Tuesday saving is most useful when the order matches the menu.

## Planning the start of the week

The two stores ask for different kinds of planning. The M&M Tuesday stocks the freezer every week, so it rewards a short and current list, written fresh each week after a look in the freezer. The Giant Tiger trip rewards a question first: a minute at the service desk, or a phone call before a long drive, turns a rumour into facts about your store. Together they cover a large share of the household spending that is not fresh groceries, and they do it before the week has barely begun.

Together they anchor the start of a discount week that continues with Value Village on Tuesday and Bulk Barn on Wednesday. A household could stock the freezer at M&M Food Market on Tuesday, add a thrift look while Tuesday is underway and finish the pantry list at Bulk Barn on Wednesday. Giant Tiger joins the week on whatever day your own store sets, if it sets one. No single trip needs to be large. The savings come from the order of the days as much as from any one purchase.

## Habits that protect the saving

Bring ID even when the cashier knows your face, because proof of age is what claims the M&M saving, and a Giant Tiger store that runs a day of its own will ask for it as well. For Giant Tiger, ask the four questions before you travel, and write down the answers, because a store can change its own day without any announcement from the chain. For M&M Food Market, make sure your Tuesday shop happens at a full store or online rather than at an Express counter. Each check takes a minute, and each one protects the saving.

That is the honest picture. One chain publishes its promise and repeats it every week. The other leaves the day to each store, and the only way to know yours is to ask. A discount you have confirmed yourself beats a discount you have only read about, every single time.
