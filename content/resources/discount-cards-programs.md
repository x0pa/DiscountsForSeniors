Most senior discounts in Canada are not automatic. Some need a free membership (like a pharmacy advantage program), some run only on a set day of the week, and some are simply a standing lower price for anyone over a certain age.

## Three kinds of programs

- **Membership programs**: sign up once, show the card or app at checkout.
- **Discount days**: the saving runs one day a week or month, like a Thursday seniors day at the pharmacy.
- **Standing senior prices**: lower fares and admission prices that apply every day, no card needed.

## At the till

Always ask. Staff do not always mention a discount, and the cashier cannot always apply one after the fact. Carry photo ID with your birth date: it is the one thing every program accepts.
