## Before you book

- Ask about senior fares and rates first: VIA Rail offers 60+ fares, and many hotel groups have senior rates that are not shown by default.
- Compare the senior rate against public promotions; sometimes the sale price wins.
- Check cancellation terms before paying.

## Before you leave

- Photo ID and health cards, plus a copy stored separately.
- Medications in original packaging, with a list of what you take.
- Chargers, glasses, and hearing aid batteries, the small things that ruin a day when forgotten.
- Tell your bank where you are travelling.

## On the road

Senior admission pricing exists at most major attractions. It is rarely advertised at the door, so ask at the ticket desk.
