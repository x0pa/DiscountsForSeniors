This entry gives you the honest answer about Jean Coutu, and it is different from most entries in this directory. Jean Coutu publishes no senior discount for the whole chain. There is no chain wide programme, no set seniors day, and no percentage that every store honours. If you have heard a set figure or a set day for Jean Coutu, it does not come from a programme that applies to all stores in Quebec, Ontario, and New Brunswick.

How to find out what your store offers

1. Visit or call your own Jean Coutu store and ask directly what offers it has for older customers, since that store sets its own offers.
2. Bring photo ID in case staff ask to confirm your age when you ask about an offer for older customers.
3. Before you pay for anything, ask staff to confirm whether any offer they described applies to your purchase, so there are no surprises at the till.

The fine print here is the structure of the chain itself. Each Jean Coutu store is owned by a pharmacist owner and sets its own offers for older customers. That is why this listing cannot give you a percentage or a day: there is none that covers the whole chain, and giving you one would be misleading.

For the same reason, offers vary from store to store. What one location offers may not be offered at another location in the same city, and an offer may change without any change to this listing. The only reliable source is the store where you actually shop, on the day you actually shop there.

Practical tip: ask once at your regular store and make a note of what they tell you, then ask again if some time has passed, since a local offer set by that store can change.
