The Salvation Army Thrift Store gives guests aged 60 and older 10% off every day at participating stores across Canada. Because the offer runs every day, you do not need to plan around one set date. You can visit when the trip suits you, take your time browsing, and still ask for the discount when you pay.

## How to claim it

Ask at the checkout, and bring proof of age with you just in case.

1. Check that the store you plan to visit takes part before you go.
2. Bring photo ID that shows your age, in case staff ask to see it.
3. Mention the discount for guests aged 60 and older before you pay.

The one clear limit is about combining offers. This discount cannot be combined with another offer. So on a store sale day, compare the two savings and take whichever one is larger for your purchase. That small bit of math at the checkout can matter, especially on a larger item. Ask staff to explain the choices if the signs or the till are not clear.

Only participating stores are included, so terms can vary from one location to the next, and they can change over time. A store near you may also run its own sale days on its own schedule. Checking the store listing before a special trip is a sensible way to confirm that the daily discount is offered there.

A practical tip is to browse with a short list of what you are looking for, then compare the price with and without each offer before you decide. The larger saving is not always the first one you see, and a calm check before you pay keeps it simple.
