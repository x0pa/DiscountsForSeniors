Every Tuesday is Senior Tuesday at Value Village. Shoppers aged 60 and older save 30% on their purchases, which is a large saving by any standard and a very large one by thrift store standards. It suits shoppers who can plan a visit for the right day and who enjoy taking time to browse before they buy.

## How to claim it

Because the terms are posted store by store, confirming them comes first.

1. Check your own store's page on the official Savers store site before you go, and confirm that the Senior Tuesday terms are posted for that store.
2. Bring photo ID that shows your age, in case staff ask to see it.
3. Mention the senior discount at the checkout before you pay, and ask staff to confirm that the 30% has been applied.

The fine print is short: the saving excludes new merchandise, which is the stock wearing red tags in the store. The clothing, housewares and other second hand goods across the floor are what the offer is aimed at. Some restrictions may apply, so if an item looks like it sits on the boundary, ask before you pay.

One honest note about where these terms live. They are posted on individual store pages on the official Savers site, not on one national policy page, and the savings block does not appear on every store page. That is why the first step above matters: your store's own page is the source that counts for your visit.

A practical tip is to go with a short list, browse at your own pace, and compare prices as you find things. A discount is worth most on items you truly want, at a price that was fair before the discount was applied.
