M&M Food Market gives shoppers aged 60 and older 10% off regular priced items every Tuesday, in store and online. A weekly day makes this easy to plan around, whether you prefer to visit a store in person or place an order from home. It suits a regular Tuesday shop for the week ahead.

## How to claim it

You claim it in the store, or through the online checkout on a Tuesday.

1. Shop on a Tuesday, in store or online, and choose regular priced items.
2. In store, mention the discount before you pay and be ready to show proof of age.
3. Online, check that the saving shows at the checkout before you pay when you place your order on Tuesday.

A few limits are worth knowing before you start. Gift cards and taxes are excluded, the saving cannot be combined with another offer, and proof of age is required. The offer also does not apply at M&M Food Market Express counters inside convenience stores, so check which kind of location you are using. And because the saving is on regular priced items, an item at a reduced price may not qualify. Ask staff if any part of your order is unclear.

Details can change over time, and the way a Tuesday order is handled online may differ from a visit in person. Check the store listing or the checkout page carefully before you finish, and confirm the discount shows before you pay. If it does not show, that is the moment to pause and ask.

A practical tip is to build your list before Tuesday and order or visit on the day itself. Compare prices as you choose, and use the 10% on the regular priced items you had already planned to buy.
