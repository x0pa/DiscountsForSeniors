Capilano Suspension Bridge Park in North Vancouver is one of the best loved attractions in British Columbia, and its published ticket prices include a senior rate. The park counts an adult as 18 to 64 and a senior as 65 and up, and in each rate class the senior price sits about $5 under the adult price. The bridge itself, the cliffwalk, and the treetop walkways all sit inside one admission, so the senior saving applies to the full visit.

How to claim it

1. Choose your visit date and check the price calendar on the park's own site, since prices are set by date and vary by season and by rate type.
2. Select the senior ticket for each guest aged 65 and up when you buy, whether you book online or pay at the gate.
3. Bring photo ID that shows your age, in case staff ask to see it at entry.

There is no single fixed daytime price at this park, so treat any one fixed figure quoted elsewhere with care. The published example that can be checked today is the Summer Twilight rate, for entry after 5 p.m., where an adult pays $59.95 and a senior pays $55.45. Daytime entry, peak days, and special event dates each follow the calendar price for that date, and the gap between the adult and senior prices stays at about $5 in the same rate class. Children aged 0 to 5 enter free.

Practical tip: an evening visit on the twilight rate pairs the lower senior price with smaller crowds, and in the winter season the canyon lights come on at dusk.
