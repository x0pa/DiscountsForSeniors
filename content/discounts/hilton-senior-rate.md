Hilton offers a senior rate to guests aged 65 and over, and the group is strict about that threshold: the published terms limit the rate to guests 65 and over only. The discount varies by hotel, and Hilton does not publish one percentage for the whole chain, so one property might trim a little and another a lot. Participating hotels can change, and blackout dates apply at some properties, which makes this a rate to check for every trip rather than a standing promise.

How to claim it

1. Search your destination and dates on Hilton's site or app and select the senior rate option where it appears.
2. Compare the senior price with the standard price for the same room and the same cancellation terms.
3. Bring proof of your age and eligibility, since the hotel can ask to see it at check in.

The rate covers a maximum of two rooms, and the second room is for family members. It applies to leisure stays only, so a trip booked and paid for by an employer falls outside the terms.

Because the size of the discount is set hotel by hotel, the senior rate is not always the lowest price on the screen. Sales that require payment in advance and member prices sometimes beat it. The saving is real only when you compare, so never book the senior rate on faith alone.

Practical tip: if the senior rate does not appear for your dates, try shifting the trip by a day or two, since blackout dates often cover single busy nights rather than whole weeks.
