Michaels offers shoppers aged 55 and older 10% off in its Canadian stores. The terms published in Michaels Canada's own coupon terms are direct about how it works: the discount is given in store, you show valid ID to prove your age, and you can use the discount once a day. There is no special day to wait for, so it can fit a project you are starting now, or a small trip to replace something you have run out of.

## How to claim it

Claiming it should be simple, with a little preparation.

1. Bring photo ID that shows your age, since the terms ask for valid ID.
2. Shop in store, gather what you need for the day, and mention the senior discount at the checkout before you pay.
3. Show your ID when staff ask for it, and check your receipt to confirm the 10% was applied.

The fine print is the part to respect here, because the exclusions list is long. The published terms leave out clearance items, Everyday Value products, Cricut, Silhouette, gift cards, books and magazines, and Christmas trees, among other lines. If you are planning a purchase around one of those, ask staff before you buy whether it qualifies, so the answer does not arrive as a surprise at the till.

Because the discount can be used once a day, it rewards a little planning. Grouping the supplies for one project into a single visit makes better use of the day's discount than two small trips would. Terms can change over time, so the coupon terms on the Michaels Canada site are the place to confirm the details before a large purchase.

A practical tip is to plan your project list before you visit, and compare prices first as well. A discount helps most when the starting price is fair and the item is one you will truly use.
