Ren's Pets, the pet supply chain with stores across Ontario and Atlantic Canada, runs a Senior's Day for customers aged 60 and over. The offer runs in store and online, and it belongs to members of Ren's Rewards, the chain's loyalty programme. Joining Ren's Rewards is free, so the membership condition costs nothing more than a few minutes at sign up.

How to claim it

1. Join Ren's Rewards if you have not already, and keep your account details handy.
2. Watch for the next Senior's Day announcement from your store or on the Ren's site, since Ren's sets and announces each date itself.
3. In store, shop on the day and show government issued photo ID if the store asks for it. Online, log in to your account before you check out, since the offer only applies to shoppers who are logged in.

Readers should know what the published terms from Ren's do not say. The terms do not state the percentage, and they do not name a fixed calendar day. Any site that quotes a firm figure or a firm weekday for this offer is adding detail that Ren's itself has not published, and this entry will not guess at either. What the terms do say is plain: the offer is valid on Senior's Day only, it cannot be combined with other discounts or promotions, and exclusions may apply.

Because each date is announced rather than fixed, the practical habit is to check before a big shop. A bag of food or litter bought a day early misses the offer entirely, while the same shop planned for the announced day earns it.

Practical tip: ask your store when the next Senior's Day falls and note the date on the calendar, then hold larger pet supply purchases for that day.
