This entry covers one city only, and it says so up front. The five IHOP restaurants in Niagara Falls, Ontario run a 55+ menu: a section of smaller portions at set prices for guests aged 55 and over. It is a priced menu, not a percentage off, so the saving comes from ordering a plate that was sized and priced for that menu in the first place. The five restaurants are run by two local operators, and both publish the offer on their own menus.

How to claim it

1. Visit one of the IHOP locations in Niagara Falls and ask for the 55+ menu if it is not handed to you.
2. Choose your plate from the 55+ section and confirm the price with your server before you order.
3. Bring identification that shows your age in case the restaurant asks, though a menu order rarely needs it.

At the Lundy's Lane restaurant, all four plates on the 55+ menu cost $14.99: the 55+ Breakfast Sampler, the 55+ Rise 'N Shine, the 55+ Cheese Omelette and the 55+ French Toast. The matching plates on the regular menu run from $16.99 to $19.99, so the saving on a single plate is real, even if it is modest. The menu from the second operator carries a Seniors 55+ section with 12 items, which gives regulars more choice. Prices are set by each operator and can differ between the five locations, so the menu in front of you is the one that counts.

No other Canadian IHOP location publishes a 55+ menu, and this entry makes no claim that they offer one. If you visit an IHOP elsewhere in Canada, ask, but do not count on finding the same section.

Practical tip: the 55+ plates suit smaller appetites well, and the price gap is widest at breakfast, which is when most readers will use this menu.
