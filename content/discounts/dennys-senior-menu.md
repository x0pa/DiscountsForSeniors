Denny's Canada offers a simple and welcome arrangement for guests aged 55 and older: a 55+ menu with smaller portions at lower prices, available every day at participating restaurants. Instead of a discount day you have to remember or a percentage you have to work out, you get a selection of breakfast, lunch and dinner plates sized and priced for senior appetites. It suits anyone who prefers a smaller plate at a smaller price, whether you are stopping in for breakfast or sitting down for an evening meal.

How to claim it

1. When you are seated, ask your server for the 55+ menu if it is not offered to you.
2. Look over the breakfast, lunch and dinner plates on that menu and choose the portion and price that suit you.
3. Order from the 55+ menu by name, and confirm the price with your server before your order goes in if anything is unclear.

The fine print is refreshingly light, but it still helps to be clear about what this offer is. It is a menu with senior pricing, not a percentage off the regular menu. The saving comes from ordering a plate that was sized and priced for the 55+ menu in the first place. It is also part of the regular menu, not a limited promotion, so you do not need to watch for a start date or an end date.

Menus and prices can still differ between restaurants and can change over time, so check the menu in front of you rather than relying on a price you remember from another Denny's or from an earlier visit. On the chain's own menus, the 55+ Club Sandwich costs $13.19 in Winnipeg and $12.79 in Calgary and Vancouver, which shows how much the same plate can move from one city to the next. If the 55+ menu is not brought to your table, that does not mean it is not available.

Practical tip: ask for the 55+ menu as soon as you sit down, every time you visit, so you can compare its plates and prices with the regular menu before you decide.

*Verified on October 18, 2026 against Denny's Canada's own published menus.*
