Wednesdays are seniors day at Bulk Barn, with a discount for shoppers aged 65 and older. If that describes you, the offer suits a regular weekly shop, because Wednesday comes around every week rather than once a month. You can plan around it without a long wait, and you can make the trip part of your usual routine.

## How to claim it

Mention the discount before you pay, and let staff guide you on how your store applies it.

1. Plan your shop for a Wednesday, and check the store listing before you go.
2. Bring photo ID that shows your age, in case staff ask to see it.
3. At the checkout, ask staff to confirm the discount before you finish paying.

This listing does not give a percentage, so do not assume a figure in advance. Ask staff what the discount covers on that day, and glance at your receipt before you leave, while any question is easy to sort out.

The fine print worth checking is simple. Ask whether the discount covers everything in your basket or only some items, and whether it can be used with another offer. Those points are not set out here, so treat the answers as something to confirm in the store, not as a promise. Asking early is always easier than asking after you have paid.

Terms can vary by location and can change over time. One store may also handle the day in its own way. If you are making a special trip, check the store listing or call ahead to confirm that Wednesday is still the day at that location.

A practical tip is to keep a running list during the week and save this shop for Wednesday when you can. Compare prices as you go, too. The best saving is the one that lowers what you pay for things you already planned to buy.
