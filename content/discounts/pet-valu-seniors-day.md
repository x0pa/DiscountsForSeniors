Pet Valu holds a Seniors Day on the last Thursday of every month, with 10% off regular priced merchandise for shoppers aged 60 and older. The offer is for members of the Your Rewards loyalty programme, which is free to join. It suits pet owners who buy supplies through the month and who can plan one monthly trip around a set day. If you shop at Pet Valu anyway, this is a simple way to pay less for the things you planned to buy.

Claiming the discount takes a little planning, so follow these steps.

1. Join Your Rewards if you are not already a member, and remember that joining is free.
2. Mark the last Thursday of the month on your calendar, because that is the Seniors Day named in this entry.
3. Bring photo ID showing your age, and tell staff before you pay that you would like the Seniors Day discount.
4. Check that the 10% has come off the regular priced items on your receipt before you leave the store.

The fine print matters in two places. First, membership matters: the discount in this entry is for rewards members, so a shopper who has not joined should not assume the saving will apply. Second, the discount is on regular priced merchandise. Members who shop online instead receive a one time code by email each month, as long as they have opted in to Pet Valu emails. Ask staff before you pay whether an item you want counts as regular priced, so the total at the till holds no surprises.

Honest notes: this entry lists Pet Valu for all provinces, with the same programme name and the same basic terms on paper. Even so, ask your local store to confirm how Seniors Day runs there, especially if you are making a special trip for it. A quick question at the counter, or a call before you leave home, can save a wasted journey.

Practical tip: keep a running list of what your pet needs during the month, then do one planned shop on the last Thursday. Planning around the day makes the 10% work harder, because more of what you already intended to buy is bought while the discount is on. Compare the final price at the till with your list.
