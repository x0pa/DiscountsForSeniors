Sandman Hotels runs one of the most generous senior offers published by a Canadian hotel group. In the chain's own wording, guests over 55 save up to 30% off at participating Sandman and Sandman Signature hotels, and the offer also includes a food and beverage gift card for the stay. Sandman is a Canadian group with hotels in cities and towns across the country, so this one is worth knowing for road trips as much as for city breaks.

How to claim it

1. Open the senior offer on Sandman's site and pick the hotel you want, since only participating hotels carry the offer.
2. Book the senior offer rate for your dates and confirm that the discount shows in the total before you pay.
3. Bring identification that shows your age, in case the front desk asks to see it when you arrive.

Two honest cautions belong with this offer. The first sits in the words up to: not every hotel and not every date reaches the full 30%, and quieter dates can price differently from busy ones. The second is that Sandman's listing does not name the amount of the gift card, and it does not name the base rate the percentage comes off. For both details, the offer page for the hotel you pick is the only reliable source, so read it before you book rather than assuming the headline figure covers everything.

The offer applies at participating hotels only, and standard taxes and fees still come on top of the room price. If the senior rate does not appear for your dates, the hotel may not be taking part, or those dates may not be included.

Practical tip: price the same room at the senior offer and at the standard rate side by side. On some dates the senior offer wins easily, and on others the gap is small.
