On the first Tuesday of each month, shoppers aged 55 and older save 10% on regular priced products at participating RONA and RONA+ stores. It is a monthly offer for readers planning home projects, repairs, or seasonal jobs around the house. If you have a shopping list building up, timing a trip for that Tuesday can lower the cost of the regular priced products on it, provided your store takes part and the products you want are not on the exclusion list.

Claiming the discount is straightforward if you prepare before you go.

1. Check that your local RONA or RONA+ store is a participating store before you make the trip.
2. Bring photo ID showing your date of birth, because staff will ask for it at the till.
3. Shop on the first Tuesday of the month, and tell staff before you pay that you are claiming the 55+ discount.
4. Ask staff to confirm which of your items qualify, and check the receipt to see that the discount was applied.

The fine print is the long exclusion list, and it deserves a careful look before a big shop. Excluded items and services include major appliances, construction lumber, asphalt shingles, gypsum panels, gift cards, installation services, and delivery services, along with many named brands. The discount also cannot be combined with other offers. A project that leans on lumber or panels may therefore save far less than the headline figure of 10% suggests.

Honest notes: the word participating does real work in this entry. Do not assume that every store is in. Confirm yours before you fill a cart or plan a project around the day. Staff at your store are also the right people to explain which brands are named in the exclusions, because this entry says many brands are named without listing each one.

Practical tip: split your list in two before you go, with regular priced products that should qualify on one side and likely excluded items on the other. Ask staff about the second group before you pay, compare the final price, and decide with clear numbers. That small bit of planning protects both the saving and your budget.
