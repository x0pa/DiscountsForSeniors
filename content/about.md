We started Discounts for Seniors with a simple frustration: senior discounts exist everywhere in Canada, but nobody lists them all in one trustworthy place. One store's discount day is another store's rumour.

So we check. Every discount in our [directory](/directory/) is verified against the retailer's own published policy, stamped with the date we last checked it, and checked again on a regular cycle. If a program ends, the listing says so.

Government pensions and benefits are a different topic, and they are not what this site is for. This site lists commercial discounts only: the money off that comes from a business choosing to charge older customers less.
